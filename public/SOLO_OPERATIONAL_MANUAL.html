Executive Summary & Platform Philosophy

SOLO is an enterprise-grade, peer-to-peer digital e-hailing platform engineered specifically for the South African transport ecosystem. Unlike traditional legacy aggregator platforms that deduct between 20% and 30% commission from driver earnings, SOLO operates on a Pure Zero-Commission Model (0.0% Take-Rate).

The SOLO Economic Formula: Drivers retain 100% of all rider fares. The platform charges no percentage cut, replacing it with a flat-rate R499.00/month SaaS subscription after an initial 7-day unrestricted trial. Section A Rider Operations & User Guide

1.1 Account Registration & Zero-Cost WhatsApp Verification

SOLO utilizes instant passwordless authentication anchored directly to the South African E.164 telecommunications standard. Riders register or sign in with their mobile telephone number:

  • Accepted Formats: 082 123 4567, 27821234567, or +27 82 123 4567.
  • Zero-Cost WhatsApp Verification (wa.me): A 6-digit cryptographic verification passcode and a direct WhatsApp deep-link are generated instantly. Riders tap "Open WhatsApp & Auto-Fill Code" to verify identity over mobile data or Wi-Fi with zero SMS gateway costs or carrier delivery delays.

1.2 PWA Mobile Installation (Android, Samsung Internet & iOS)

SOLO is delivered as a Progressive Web Application (PWA). It requires no app store downloads and works in areas with constrained mobile bandwidth.

  • Android & Samsung Internet: Open the platform in Google Chrome or Samsung Internet. An automatic banner prompts "Install SOLO App — Add to Home Screen". Tapping "Install Now" installs the app in standalone mode. In Samsung Internet, drivers can also use Menu → "Add page to" → "App screen".
  • Apple iOS Devices: Open in Safari, tap the Share button (square with arrow up), scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then tap "Add".

1.3 Booking a Ride & Commercial Map Routing

SOLO uses high-resolution Mapbox Commercial Geocoding and Matrix Routing. When setting pickup and dropoff points, the system calculates the optimal route and guarantees an upfront fixed price: 

Fare (ZAR) = Maximum(R30.00, R20.00 + [Distance in km * R10.00])

Example: A 6.2 km trip between Camps Bay and the V&A Waterfront costs exactly R82.00. There are no surprise surge pricing multipliers or booking surcharges.

1.4 Driver Matching, 15km PostGIS Radius & Multi-Driver Decline Pool

When you tap "Request SOLO Ride", the PostGIS spatial engine scans for online, verified drivers within a 15-kilometer radius.

Multi-Driver Pool Availability: If an individual driver declines your ride request, your booking is NOT cancelled. The trip remains actively available in the dispatch pool for all other registered online drivers in your area until accepted.

1.5 Boarding Safety & The Mandatory 4-Digit PIN

CRITICAL SAFETY PROTOCOL: Every trip generates an anti-impersonation 4-digit PIN (e.g. 4892). When the vehicle arrives, check the registration plate, enter the vehicle, and give your 4-digit PIN to the driver. The driver cannot start the meter without this PIN. Never enter an unverified vehicle.

1.6 Payment Settlement & Tax Receipts

Payment is authorized via Paystack using 3D-Secure South African bank cards. When the driver completes the trip, the payment is captured automatically. Riders immediately receive a link to their SARS-compliant tax invoice at /receipt/[tripId] with itemized fare lines and 1-click PDF download for expense claims.

1.7 Offline Dead Zones & SOS Emergency Hotlines

If cellular network fails during a journey (e.g., mountain passes or underground parking), the SOLO PWA automatically engages offline mode. The active trip safety screen allows 1-tap emergency calling to official South African emergency numbers:

  • SAPS (Police Flying Squad): 10111
  • National Ambulance & Fire: 10177
  • Universal Cellular Emergency: 112

Section B Driver Operations & Partner Guide

2.1 Statutory NLTA Compliance & Requirements

In accordance with the National Land Transport Act (Act No. 5 of 2009), all drivers operating on the SOLO platform must register as independent micro-enterprises and provide proof of statutory compliance:

  1. South African Driving Licence (Front & Back): Valid Code B, EB, or C1 licence card.
  2. Professional Driving Permit (PrDP - Category P): Mandatory passenger transport permit.
  3. Certificate of Roadworthiness (CoR): Issued by an accredited testing station within the last 6 months.
  4. Vehicle Registration (RC1) & Commercial Insurance: Proving the vehicle is legally registered and insured.

2.2 KYC Document Submission Protocol & System Resilience

Upload high-resolution scans or photos of the 4 statutory documents at /driver/register. Documents are stored in an encrypted, private storage bucket with strict access controls. The registration engine incorporates database-level user creation pre-emption to eliminate document upload errors.

2.3 SaaS Subscription Model (7-Day Trial & R499/mo)

SOLO does not take 25% of your earnings. Instead:

  • 7-Day Free Trial: Once approved, you enjoy 7 full days of unlimited dispatches with zero platform fees.
  • R499.00 / month Flat SaaS Fee: After the trial, maintain full platform access for a flat R499/month billed through Paystack. You keep 100% of every ride fare you generate.
  • Platform Founder Exemption: Platform founder Allan Boraine (allanboraine@gmail.com) holds lifetime free driver access with zero subscription charges and permanent 0% commission.

2.5 Single-Device Active Concurrency Policy

Strict 1-Device Rule: A driver account can only be active online on ONE device at a time. Every device generates a persistent hardware-backed ID. When going online on Device B, Device A is immediately preempted, deactivated with an on-screen notice, and barred from dispatch until re-authorized.

2.6 Daily Operating Routine & Decline Handling

Open the SOLO Driver app and toggle "Go Online". When a ride is booked within 15 km, your device sounds your selected tone. You see pickup address, destination, route distance, and guaranteed Rand fare. Tap "Accept Trip" to receive navigation routing. If you tap "Decline", the trip is dismissed only from your screen while remaining active for other drivers in your area.

2.7 Driver Notification Alerts & Native Mobile Device Sound Customization

SOLO integrates directly with your smartphone's native notification and audio system:

  • Native Mobile Device Notification Tones: Drivers can set and customize their alert sound directly within their phone's operating system settings (Android Settings → Apps / Notifications → Chrome / Samsung Internet / SOLO → Notification sound, or iOS Settings → Notifications). Any ringtone or custom sound file saved on your phone can be chosen as the dispatch alert tone.
  • Foreground Acoustic Dispatch Chime: When the app is open in the foreground, incoming dispatches sound an ascending acoustic chime (D5 → A5 → D6) synthesized via the Web Audio API.
  • Tactile Haptic Vibration Pattern: Every incoming dispatch triggers a tactile vibration sequence ([250ms, 100ms, 250ms, 100ms, 450ms]) to alert drivers even in noisy traffic conditions.
  • Service Worker Push Notifications: Dispatches use high-priority Service Worker notifications (requireInteraction: true, renotify: true), ensuring notifications play the user's selected native notification tone even when the app is in the background.

2.8 Mobile Ergonomics & Samsung Internet Optimization

The driver interface is designed for one-handed in-vehicle dashboard mounting:

  • Centered Navigation: Navigation bar icons and action buttons are centered for effortless thumb reach.
  • Samsung Internet Compatibility: Uses dynamic viewport units (dvh) to prevent browser address bars and Samsung One UI navigation bars from covering bottom sheets or action buttons.
  • Collapsible Dispatch Sheet: Swipe down to expand the real-time radar map, or swipe up to view earnings and settings.

2.9 Arrival & PIN Verification

When you arrive at the pickup point, tap "I Have Arrived" to alert the passenger. When the passenger enters your vehicle, ask for their 4-digit SOLO safety PIN and input it into your terminal. Once confirmed, tap "Start Trip". At the destination, tap "Complete Trip" to immediately trigger payment settlement.

2.10 Driver SARS Tax Guidance

Tax Transparency for Drivers: Because SOLO drivers receive 100% gross fares, drivers operate as independent contractors for SARS purposes. Fuel, vehicle maintenance, insurance, cell data, and your R499 monthly SOLO SaaS fee are legitimate tax-deductible business expenses. Drivers can consider registering for SARS Turnover Tax if annual turnover is under R1,000,000.

Section C Administrator Operations & Governance

3.1 KYC Review Engine (`/admin/compliance`)

The central administration console at /admin/compliance provides real-time oversight of driver applicant queues across South Africa. The telemetry dashboard displays:

  • Total Drivers: Total driver records in the platform database.
  • Pending Review: Applications waiting for compliance officer document inspection.
  • Verified & Active: Approved drivers eligible for real-time dispatch matching.
  • Action Needed: Applications where documents were rejected or need re-submission.

3.2 1-Hour Ephemeral Signed URLs for KYC Documents

To comply with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA Act No. 4 of 2013), driver identity documents and PrDP certificates are never exposed via public URLs. The compliance portal dynamically generates 1-hour signed URLs (`createSignedUrl(path, 3600)`) directly from the private `driver-documents` bucket for inline inspection.

Statutory Rejection Codes: When rejecting a document, administrators must select a preset reason (e.g. EXPIRED_CARD, NO_PRDP_ENDORSEMENT, ROADWORTHY_EXPIRED, VIN_MISMATCH). Approving all 4 statutory documents automatically sets is_verified = true and dispatches an approval notification to the driver.

3.3 Dual-Window Simulator (`/admin/simulator`)

The interactive simulator allows compliance officers and developers to simulate full trip lifecycles in side-by-side device frames:

  • Metro Route Presets: Pre-programmed routes in Cape Town, Johannesburg, Durban, and Pretoria.
  • Speed Control: Execute journeys at 1x (real-time), 2x, or 5x speed multipliers.
  • 5-Minute TTL Driver Telematics: Real drivers only, with stale driver records expiring after 5 minutes of inactivity to prevent ghost markers.
  • Interactive PIN & Chat: Test the 4-digit anti-impersonation PIN verification and real-time messaging.
  • Live Telemetry Drawer: Inspect PostGIS 15km matching logs, Supabase WebSockets, VAPID Web Push payloads, and Paystack settlement events.

3.4 Paystack HMAC SHA512 Webhooks

All billing operations (trip fare settlement and R499/mo driver subscriptions) are validated cryptographically at /api/paystack-webhook. Incoming webhooks are verified against PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY using HMAC-SHA512. Forged payloads or incorrect signatures are rejected with HTTP 401.

3.5 PostGIS Proximity Matching Algorithms

Spatial matching runs on PostgreSQL PostGIS using geodesic distance queries (ST_DWithin) indexed with spatial GIST indexes. Only drivers who are online (is_online = true), verified (is_verified = true), and with active SaaS subscriptions are included in dispatch candidate pools.

3.6 Web Push VAPID Protocol Infrastructure

Background alerts for bookings, driver arrivals, and payments follow the RFC 8291 / RFC 8292 standard. Stale subscriptions that return HTTP 404 or HTTP 410 are automatically pruned from the push_subscriptions table to optimize database performance.

3.7 Dispute Resolution & Audit Trails

In case of passenger or driver queries, administrators can cross-reference the trip ID in Supabase logs, inspect the exact GPS breadcrumb path, check the timestamp of the 4-digit PIN confirmation, and review the Paystack transaction authorization code.
